How I scoped it:
- From 16 maintainer pain points, I picked the ones an LLM helps with and that
  fit the budget, then scoped the tool to three questions: what changed, whose
  move it is, and what themes emerge.
- An eval against my hand labels first scored 14%, exposing a definition gap
  (an open PR means it's the maintainer's move). Fixing that and fetching linked
  PRs raised it to 94%.

Deliberately did NOT build:
- LangGraph: the pipeline is a fixed sequence.
- Routing/assignment: needs team ownership data.
- PR triage: a different workflow (CI, reviews).
- Write actions: read-only by design.
- Duplicate detection: needs the full backlog stored.

With more time:
- Separate noise from signal (simple questions, wrong-repo reports, spam,
  incomplete reports) into its own section with suggested actions.
